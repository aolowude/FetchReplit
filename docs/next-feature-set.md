# Next Feature Set — Engagement & Retention

## Core Goal
Make the app addictive for learning about health, food, and nutrition — sticky, personalised, and rewarding.

---

## 1. Feedback Loop on Suggestions
**Priority: Highest**

Add interaction with every suggestion card:
- Thumbs up / thumbs down
- "Not for me"
- "Why this?" — expand to show the memory facts powering the recommendation

**Why:** This is the only way the AI personalises for the individual user. Without it, the memory system is wasted and the app feels like a black box.

---

## 2. Bite-Sized Nutrition Micro-Lessons on Scan
**Priority: High**

Every scan result becomes an education moment. Not just the numbers — contextual insights:
- "This is high in saturated fat — here's why that matters for heart health"
- "A lower-fat alternative based on your preferences"
- Tie facts to things the user already cares about

**Why:** Education is the addictive hook. Users build genuine nutrition literacy and become dependent on the app to keep learning.

---

## 3. Daily Streak + Achievement System
**Priority: High**

- Visible streak counter on the home page — "Day 7 — keep it going!"
- Badges tied to real actions:
  - "First scan"
  - "Week warrior"
  - "Fridge stocked"
  - "Recipe explorer"
  - "Used 3 expiring items"
- Pair with re-engagement push notifications — "Your streak is at risk tomorrow!"

**Why:** Proven retention mechanic. The app already has all the underlying actions — just need to surface them as milestones.

---

## 4. Weekly Challenges
**Priority: Medium-High**

Auto-generated based on user profile goals and current fridge state:
- "Use 3 items expiring this week"
- "Hit your protein target 4 out of 7 days"
- "Log your meals for 5 consecutive days"

Completing challenges earns streak days and badges.

**Why:** Turns passive tracking into active participation and gives users something to do in the app beyond just scanning.

---

## 5. Daily Evening Recap
**Priority: Medium**

End-of-day summary delivered at a configurable time:
- "Today you hit 78% of your calorie target"
- "You were 15g short on protein — here's a high-protein snack idea from your fridge"
- Quick wins and misses of the day

**Why:** Creates a second daily open, reinforces the learning loop, and gives users a habitual reason to check back in the evening.
